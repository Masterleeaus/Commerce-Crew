# Capability Map

## Commerce
Catalogue, inventory, cart, checkout, pricing, tax, shipping, fulfilment, payments, BNPL, native orders, returns and rental/hire receivables.

## Conversational intelligence
Three-role commerce, conversational context, failure translation, budget locks, conversational cards, customer communications, message triage, support approvals and human handoff.

## Marketplaces
Assisted shopping, marketplace search, order import, governed writes, approvals, rollback, rate-limit governance, bulk operations, dry runs, partial rollback, listing intelligence, compliance, brand voice, evidence-backed claims and safe listing rewrites.

## Inventory coordination
Marketplace inventory reconciliation, inventory conflict resolution, reservation-aware channel allocation and conflict history.

## Security and authority
Credential vaulting, rotation/revocation, signed session authority, tenant isolation and fail-closed public-session handling.

## Operations and reliability
Registered scheduling, tenant-aware queues, durable payment webhooks, retries, dead letters, provider circuit breakers, lifecycle management, unified native/external order workbench, settlement reconciliation and exception management.

## Channels and supporting modules
WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, voice, voice calls, booking, customer tags and reviews.
